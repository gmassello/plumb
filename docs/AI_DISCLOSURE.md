# AI disclosure

What in this entry was made with AI tools, besides Plumb's own use of GitLab Duo.

## Demo video

- **Presenter:** the face and voice in the video are an AI avatar and a cloned voice of the author, Germán Massello, made with HeyGen from his own photos and recordings. The video says so on its closing slide and in its last spoken line.
- **Script:** written by the author with the help of an AI coding assistant. Every figure it states comes from [`docs/RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) or [`docs/CLAIMS.md`](CLAIMS.md).
- **Screen recording:** a real run of the loop on the live project, captured from the browser. Waiting time is sped up; nothing on screen is mocked.
- **Opening sequence and closing slide:** generated from files in [`video/`](../video/) with ffmpeg and headless Chrome. The opening's sound is synthesised from oscillators and noise.
